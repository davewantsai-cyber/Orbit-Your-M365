## Fair Use and Research Intent

ORBIT was created for legitimate cybersecurity research, professional security assessment, and educational purposes. It automates the use of publicly available, open-source tools published by authoritative organisations (including CISA, the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency) and the wider security research community.

ORBIT does not:
- Exploit vulnerabilities or weaknesses.
- Modify, delete, or exfiltrate data from assessed environments.
- Perform any action beyond reading configuration data through official Microsoft APIs.

The integrated tools (ScubaGear, Maester, Monkey365, ROADtools, Hawk) each operate under their own licences and terms. ORBIT does not modify, redistribute, or fork any of them — it invokes them through their published command-line interfaces.
